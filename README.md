## 🎯 Business Objective

Analyze e-commerce sales performance and identify patterns that can support decisions around **product strategy, pricing, inventory and marketing**.

## 🖥️ Dashboard

The dashboard contains four analytical views:

**01 — Overview**  
KPI cards, city/state sales comparison, total-amount trend and filters for ship state, status, category and fulfilment.

**02 — Product**  
Product-level cards and visual exploration of individual products.

**03 — Product View**  
Detailed product performance with units, sale amount, reviews and time-based trends.

**04 — Detailed Product Analysis**  
Additional product-level performance exploration.

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Maharashtra** leads state-level sales in the dashboard, followed by Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.
- **Bengaluru** is the strongest city by sales among the displayed cities.
- Total sales amount reaches **89.08M** across the analyzed period.
- The overview shows meaningful fluctuations in sales amount from April through June 2022.
